Privacy Policy – Fairmont Credit Partners

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

Overview

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Fairmont Credit Partners (“Company”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you visit our website, submit an application, or interact with our services.

By accessing or using our website and services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Mobile Messaging Privacy

Fairmont Credit Partners is committed to protecting your privacy. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

  • Information Collection: We collect your phone number only when you provide it via our web forms. This information is used solely to communicate with you regarding your funding requests.
  • Opt-Out: You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Just text “STOP” to our number. After you send the SMS message “STOP” to us, we will send you an SMS message to confirm that you have been unsubscribed.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service. Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency may vary.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect different types of information depending on how you interact with our website or services.

  • Personal Information: Data that can identify you directly or indirectly, such as full name, email address, phone number, mailing address, business name/details, and IP address/device information.
  • Business & Financial Information: If you submit a funding request or application, we may collect information such as business revenue, financial records, bank account information, business ownership details, credit-related information, and government identification numbers (where required).
  • Automatically Collected Information: When you visit our website, we may collect data including browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, and website usage behavior.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for several purposes, including:

  • Processing and evaluating funding applications
  • Verifying identity and preventing fraud
  • Communicating with you about your application or account
  • Providing customer support
  • Improving our website, products, and services
  • Sending service-related updates or promotional communications
  • Complying with legal or regulatory requirements

We may also use aggregated or anonymized data for analytics and business insights.

To the extent we use automated tools or algorithms to assist in evaluating funding applications or assessing creditworthiness, such tools are used to support, not replace, human review. Where required by applicable law, you may have the right to request human review of any automated decision that has a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. However, we may share information with trusted parties when necessary, including:

  • Service Providers: Third-party companies that help us operate our business, such as payment processors, data hosting providers, customer support platforms, and marketing/analytics providers.
  • Funding Partners: If you apply for financing, your information may be shared with lenders, funding providers, or financial institutions that help evaluate or fulfill your request.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to comply with laws, respond to legal requests/court orders, protect the rights and safety of our company or users, or prevent fraud or illegal activity.

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, and similar tracking technologies to remember user preferences, analyze website traffic, improve website functionality, and provide marketing or advertising services. Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings. Our website does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. Where required by law, we honor recognized opt-out preference signals such as the Global Privacy Control (GPC).

5. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure, such as secure servers, data encryption, access controls, and monitoring systems. However, no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, meeting legal or regulatory obligations, resolving disputes, and enforcing agreements. When information is no longer needed, it may be securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites they visit.
