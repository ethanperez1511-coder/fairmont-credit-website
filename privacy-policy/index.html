FCP Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

FCP ("FCP," "we," "us," or "our") provides merchant cash advance brokerage services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle information when you visit our website, request assistance, or communicate with our team.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

We collect information you provide when requesting our services or communicating with us. This may include your name, business name, email address, telephone number, business and application information, supporting documents, and correspondence.

For SMS communications, we maintain your mobile number, messaging preferences, consent records, and relevant message history to provide support and manage your communication preferences.

Our inquiry form submits your name, company, email address, telephone number, and message to a hosted database we use to manage inquiries. Our application form is an embedded form hosted by our customer relationship management provider, which processes the application information you enter and transmits it to us, including by email.

Information collected automatically

Our website is served by a third-party hosting provider that automatically records standard server log information for each request, including your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested, referring pages, and the date and time of your visit.

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Ads conversion tracking. These Google services set or read cookies and similar identifiers in your browser to measure website activity, attribute form submissions to advertising campaigns, and support advertising and remarketing. Information collected through these services is processed by Google in accordance with its own privacy terms.

Our pages load fonts from Google's font service, so Google receives the request information needed to deliver those files, including your IP address. Our embedded application form is loaded from our customer relationship management provider, which may also set cookies or similar identifiers on the pages where it appears.

Our cookie notice records your accept-or-decline choice in your browser's local storage so that the notice is not shown again on that browser.

We do not use website analytics or advertising technologies other than those described above.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide personal guidance.
  • Assist with requested funding applications and related documentation.
  • Provide merchant support, follow-ups, and customer success assistance.
  • Coordinate communications about active requests.
  • Maintain communication preferences and consent records.
  • Measure website and advertising performance.
  • Protect our services, resolve disputes, and meet applicable recordkeeping and legal obligations.

3. Sharing Information

When you request assistance with a funding application, we may share relevant business and application information with funding providers to evaluate and process your requested transaction, subject to any required authorization.

Service providers may process information on our behalf to support our website, business operations, and customer communications. This includes our website host, our customer relationship management and form provider, our email delivery provider, our hosted database provider, and the advertising and tag management services described in Section 1. We may also disclose information when required by law or valid legal process. These disclosures remain subject to the SMS-specific protections below.

FCP does not sell or share consumer personal information, including mobile phone numbers, with third parties or affiliates for marketing, promotional, or lead-generation purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties. Consent to receive messages from FCP does not authorize another business to send you messages.

4. SMS Communications

With your consent, FCP sends merchant-support text messages, including personal guidance, follow-ups, responses to questions, and customer success assistance.

Message frequency varies based on your interactions and support needs. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe from SMS messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or call (786) 600-2724. We honor opt-out requests and retain the information needed to maintain your messaging preferences.

SMS participation is voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing a product or service. Additional messaging terms are available in our Terms of Service.

5. Information Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, maintain consent and opt-out records, resolve disputes, and satisfy applicable legal requirements.

6. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

You may withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP or contacting our support team.

You may decline cookies through our cookie notice, and you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also manage Google advertising personalization through Google's own ad settings. Declining or blocking cookies does not affect your ability to contact us or submit a request.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or practices change. The current version and last-updated date will be published on this page. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
